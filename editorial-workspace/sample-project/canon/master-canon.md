# Master Canon (SAMPLE DATA)

> Demo material for testing the workspace. Not part of The Long Picture.

## Setting

The story takes place in Harrow Bay, a fishing town on a cold northern coast, across one winter.

## Timeline

- **October:** Iris Hale returns to Harrow Bay after eleven years away.
- **November:** The lighthouse is sold to a developer.
- **December:** The storm. The harbour wall fails on the night of the 14th.

## Fixed facts

- The lighthouse has been dark since 1998.
- Iris's mother, Ruth Hale, died in the spring before the story begins.
