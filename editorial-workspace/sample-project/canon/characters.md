# Characters (SAMPLE DATA)

> Demo material for testing the workspace. Not part of The Long Picture.

## Iris Hale

Photographer, thirty-four. Left Harrow Bay at twenty-three. Precise, guarded, funny when cornered.
Never photographs people she loves.

## Tomas Reyes

Harbourmaster. Iris's oldest friend. Speaks little, notices everything. Has a daughter, June, aged nine.

## Ruth Hale

Iris's mother (deceased). Kept the lighthouse logbooks long after the light went dark.
