# The Return

The last bus to Harrow Bay ran at six, and Iris was the only passenger by the time it reached the coast road.

She found the key under the third stone of the garden wall, where it had always been, and the door opened before she could use it.

* * *

Tomas was at the harbour. He looked at her the way he looked at weather.

"You came," he said, and nothing else.
